FAQ

Common questions.

What areas do you service?

We're based on and primarily serve Auckland's North Shore. If you're just outside that area, get in touch anyway — we're always happy to see if we can help.

How much does a clean cost?

It depends on the size of the property, the type of glass or surface, and how often you'd like it done. Send us the details through the form and we'll give you a fair, free quote before any work starts.

Do I need to be home during the clean?

Not necessarily — as long as we can access the areas that need cleaning. We'll confirm access arrangements with you when we book the job.

Can you do one-off cleans, or only ongoing contracts?

Both. Plenty of our customers book a one-off clean, and others prefer a regular schedule (weekly, monthly, or seasonal). Let us know what suits you.

How quickly can you fit me in?

We'll always give you our earliest availability when we send your quote. Turnaround depends on the season and current bookings, but we move fast where we can.
